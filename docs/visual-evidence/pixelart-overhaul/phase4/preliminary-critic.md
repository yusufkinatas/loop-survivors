# Phase 4 preliminary independent critique

Date: 2026-07-18

Direction: Arcade Reliquary
Result: **REVISE / FAIL**

The independent critic inspected all 29 first-pass lossless 640 × 360 family
sheets at original resolution. The first pass was crisp and cohesive, but it
did not satisfy the binding silhouette, semantic-color, and evolved-structure
rules. This failed record is preserved rather than replaced by the corrected
review. Its superseded sheets and gameplay captures are under
`first-pass/production-art/` and `first-pass/gameplay/`; the corrected evidence
is under the top-level `production-art/` and `gameplay/` directories.

## Required response

- Redraw the Crypt Spider as a low, wide abdomen with four stepped leg pairs
  and a readable alternating gait.
- Separate zombie, skeleton, ghost, both golems, and the Dusk/Ember moths by
  outer contour in normal and grayscale views.
- Redesign Kick, Clap, Snare, Hi-hat, Lead, Arp, and Chord evolutions so their
  structures differ from the normal form rather than only size or repetition.
- Restore all XP tiers to cyan faceted diamonds with distinct split marks;
  strengthen the music-note stamp and make collection/celebration effects
  unmistakably non-pickup effects.
- Correct the extension cable, recovery tape, mirrorball, evolution,
  weapon-pedestal, heart, and XP UI glyph grammar.
- Lower the gameplay-role cyan/magenta priority of the rune dais, crystals,
  and banner.
- Regenerate complete normal, grayscale, protanopia, deuteranopia, and
  tritanopia evidence plus pivot/boundary and unlabeled combat proofs before a
  fresh independent re-review.
