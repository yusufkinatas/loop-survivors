# Trigger density, derived from the shipped ladders

Generated by `npx tsx docs/baseline/density.ts`. Every number is read from
`src/audio/ladders/house.ts` and `src/game/weapons/levels.ts` — nothing here
is hand-maintained.

A **trigger** is one musical-grid event: a sound in the mix and one attack in
the world. Density is triggers per bar. The grid is 16 steps (16th notes) per
bar; at this track's 122 BPM a bar lasts 1.97 seconds.

## Does anything ever thin out?

**No.** Across all eight roles and all four stages, no rung fires fewer
triggers per bar than the rung below it. There is no level-up anywhere in the
game that makes anything sparser.

## How full the bar gets

| all eight roles at | triggers/bar | grid steps used |
|---|---|---|
| stage 1 | 19.50 | 8/16 |
| stage 2 | 27.25 | 11/16 |
| stage 3 | 35.00 | 12/16 |
| stage 4 | 54.00 | 16/16 |

A build holds **5 weapons** and **6 passives**, so eight at once is
unreachable — but at the top stage the eight roles between them put something
on every one of the sixteen steps. There is no empty slot left for an arrival.

| sparsest possible | clap, snare, kick, bass, lead | 11.50 | **22.00** | ×1.91 | 13/16 |

The sparsest maxed build the catalog permits is the floor on late-run density.
A player who wants to strip back cannot get below it: the only lever on density
is refusing level-ups, which costs damage and buys nothing musically.
